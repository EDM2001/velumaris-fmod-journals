Table Sheet Refit

This is tomorrow's S144 sheet as it's stored in Foundry right now, drawn by the new module's own stylesheet and script. Only Foundry itself is faked, so this is the look and the behaviour you'll get in the GM Screen drawer.

  • Tick a secret. In Foundry the tick is saved on the sheet and survives a re-push.
  • Tap a scene to make it current. The earlier ones dim, and the Read aloud page next to it marks and scrolls to that scene's beats.
  • Hover a name, or tap it on a phone, for the portrait and the first line of the NPC note. A click opens the page.
  • The map chips on a scene line: the name views that map for you only. Players shows it to the table, and only on a second tap.
  • Hover a ceiling in the party grid to see what it's made of.
  • Tap a spare name when you use one. It lights up and stays lit, so afterwards you can see who you invented tonight.
Foundry theme The sheet's cell

On a wide screen the two cells sit side by side, as they do in your Quick Ref tab, and each scrolls on its own at the height of your drawer (60% of the screen). On a phone they stack.

Give the sheet the whole drawer?

Measured at your drawer's size: sharing the Quick Ref tab with Read aloud, the sheet is about one and a half drawer-heights tall at the start of the night, and it shrinks as scenes fold away. With the whole width it gets a third column and comes close to one screen. Try both with The sheet's cell above, on a wide screen.

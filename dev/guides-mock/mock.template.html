GM guides · before Foundry sees them

Gardhu’s guide and the Eden guide

Both guides as they would be stored in Foundry, drawn by the module’s own stylesheet and script inside a stand-in for your GM Screen. Only Foundry itself is faked. The counters start where Gardhu’s sheet stands tonight.

  • Tap a counter. In Foundry it spends on Gardhu’s sheet itself, the same copy the Stream Deck spends from, so both always agree. Tap a lit pip to spend it, an empty one to give it back. A recharge chip flips between ready and spent.
  • Tick a trigger as it happens. Clear ticks empties them for the next time the fight runs.
  • The buttons along the top view the scene for you only, open his sheet, or open the full Crossing Out note.
  • In Eden Arc, tap + or − on Attention, and hover a beat for its played tap. After the session I read those taps and check them against the journal before anything goes into the vault.
  • Rings already walked fold shut. Tap a ring’s name to open or close it.

Are these the guides?

If they are, here is what happens, all before Saturday’s session and after the other sessions have cleared it:

  1. I deploy the module update. It needs an F5 in your browser, no restart.
  2. I push both guides into a GM Guides journal folder, GM-only.
  3. I re-push the S144 Table Sheet so it carries tonight’s fight. After your F5, Foundry offers once to fill Encounters with Gardhu’s guide, and once to put the Eden guide in Eden Arc.

The six grey tabs are only there for show. Links inside the pages open their Foundry pages; here they just say what they would open.
